About

Hi! I'm Paula Atienza.

I'm a designer based in Madrid, originally from a small town in Extremadura. My work lives at the intersection of digital product design, interior design, branding and typography.

Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to lead impactful projects across Europe, with key experiences in Amsterdam. My portfolio spans from strategic design for global industry leaders like IKEA to user-centric product work for Spanish startups like InnoCard.

Beyond corporate work, I am involved in the design and tech ecosystem: I support Technovation Girls, write for UX Planet on Medium, and run “typocean”, a project documenting local commercial logotypes.

As a small footnote, that same curiosity for detail led me to graduate with 8 academic distinctions during my double degree. A dedication to rigor that I now bring to every digital and physical space I design.

If you'd like to discuss a project or interview opportunities, feel free to drop me a line!
