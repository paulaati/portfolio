About

Hi! I'm Paula Atienza. I’m a designer based in Madrid, originally from a small town in Extremadura. My work lives at the intersection of digital product design, interior design, branding and typography.

Throughout my career, I’ve had the opportunity to lead impactful projects across Europe, including key professional experiences in Amsterdam. My portfolio spans from strategic design for global industry leaders like IKEA to user-centric product for spanish startups like InnoCard.

Beyond corporate work, I am involved in the design and tech ecosystem: I actively participate and support initiatives like Technovation Girls to promote women in tech, write for UX Planet on Medium and run "typocean", a personal project focused on documenting local commercial logotypes to build design culture and preserve typographic history.

As a small footnote, that same curiosity for detail led me to graduate with 8 academic distinctions during my double degree. A dedication to rigor that I now bring to every digital and physical space I design.

If you'd like to discuss a project or interview opportunities, feel free to drop me a line!
